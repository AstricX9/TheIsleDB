# Allosaurus

## Game Description

Allosaurus is a large carnivore in The Isle: Evrima. It is a fast, aggressive predator that relies on pressure, positioning, and timing rather than pure strength. It excels at chasing prey and forcing mistakes through stamina drain and coordinated attacks.

**Sources:** [Evrima Quick Guide - Allosaurus](https://www.evrimaquickguide.com/playables/quick-facts-carnivores/allosaurus); local character assets and call or animation exports. No Core balance export was found for this species in the current dump.

---

## Overview

Allosaurus rewards persistence, adaptability, and smart engagement. It is especially dangerous in groups or prolonged encounters, where repeated pressure can drain a target's stamina and create openings.

---

## Survival Tips

- Pick fights where your movement and size class are favored.
- Avoid burning all stamina before the target is committed.
- Disengage early when larger animals or third parties arrive.
