# Allosaurus

## Game Description

Allosaurus is a fast and aggressive predator that relies on pressure, positioning, and timing rather than pure strength. It excels at chasing down prey and forcing mistakes through stamina drain and coordinated attacks, making it especially dangerous in groups or prolonged encounters.

**Sources:** [Evrima Quick Guide - Allosaurus](https://www.evrimaquickguide.com/playables/quick-facts-carnivores/allosaurus); local character assets and call or animation exports. No Core balance export was found for this species in the current dump.

---

## Overview

Allosaurus has a three-member pack limit and reaches full growth in approximately ten hours. Its pounce can pin lighter targets, while coordinated pounces can help pressure heavier prey. Claw attacks apply stackable bleed, making sustained pressure a core part of its playstyle.

---

## Survival Tips

- Pick fights where your movement and size class are favored.
- Avoid burning all stamina before the target is committed.
- Disengage early when larger animals or third parties arrive.
