# Carnotaurus

## Game Description

Carnotaurus is a medium carnivore in The Isle: Evrima. Medium-sized theropod dinosaur. Its local balance export is available, so growth and raw movement values are calculated from the exported curves.

**Source:** Local character assets, call assets, and Core balance exports.

---

## Overview

Carnotaurus is listed in the local Evrima export as a medium carnivore with fast speed and moderate difficulty.

## Survival Tips

- Pick fights where your movement and size class are favored.
- Avoid burning all stamina before the target is committed.
- Disengage early when larger animals or third parties arrive.
