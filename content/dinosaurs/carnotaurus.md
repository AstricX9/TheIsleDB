# Carnotaurus

## Game Description

Carnotaurus is a fast, medium-sized carnivorous theropod built for ambush hunting and hit-and-run pressure. Its speed and acceleration let it close distance quickly, while its horns and bite make it a threatening close-range predator.

## Overview

Carnotaurus is one of the fastest playable dinosaurs in The Isle, with strong burst mobility and reliable knockout pressure.

## Survival Tips

- Your speed and instant acceleration is your greatest asset — use it to your advantage.
- Avoid animals larger than yourself when possible.
- Use your charge to quickly close distance to prey.
- You're more likely to survive in a small group (2–3 players).
- Avoid sprinting when below ~60% stamina.
- Rest in open areas to better spot approaching threats.
- Use speed advantages to disengage or retreat into jungles to lose pursuers.
