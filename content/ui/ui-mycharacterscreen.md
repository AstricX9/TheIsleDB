## The My Character Screen

The character screen is the screen you see when pressing {{kbd|TAB}} on your keyboard.

## Current My Character Screen

![left|lg|In-game nutrition panel|The current My Character Screen. Showcasing the renamed diets as Beta, Gamma and Alpha](/assets/images/diets-and-nutrients/current_mycharacterscreen.png)

Currently the diet icons are now Alpha <img src="/assets/images/Neural_UI_Alpha_Nutrient.png" alt="Alpha icon" data-no-viewer="true" class="no-viewer inline-icon-image invert-image"> (as Carbohydrates), Gamma <img src="/assets/images/Neural_UI_Gamma_Nutrient.png" alt="Gamma icon" data-no-viewer="true" class="no-viewer inline-icon-image invert-image"> (as Lipids), and Beta <img src="/assets/images/Neural_UI_Beta_Nutrient.png" alt="Beta icon" data-no-viewer="true" class="no-viewer inline-icon-image invert-image"> (as Proteins).
